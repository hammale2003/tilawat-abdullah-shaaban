Privacy Policy

تلاوات عبد الله أحمد شعبان — last updated 14 November 2026

The short version

This app collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

What the app stores

Everything the app remembers is stored only in your own browser, and is deleted when you remove the app from your home screen or clear the site's data:

  • which recitation you last listened to, and where you had reached in it
  • recitations you have bookmarked
  • your playback settings (speed, repeat mode, theme)

None of this is ever transmitted anywhere. We cannot see it.

Network use

The app connects to the internet for one purpose only: to fetch the app itself and the recitation audio files. Both are served by GitHub Pages from the same address as this page. No personal information is sent with these requests beyond what any ordinary web page request involves. GitHub's own privacy statement governs those requests: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions at all: no notifications, location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Children

The app contains no advertising, no purchases, no user-generated content and no account. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

The recordings

Every recitation comes from the reciter's official channel, and only from the new recordings it publishes there. The older recordings the channel has disowned are not included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new date at the top.
